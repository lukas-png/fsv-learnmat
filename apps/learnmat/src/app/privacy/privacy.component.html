Privacy policy

As of October 2026. The German version is legally binding.

Controller

The study material distribution is run by the Computer Science student council. The controller under the GDPR is the student body (Studierendenschaft) of the Stiftung Universität Hildesheim, represented by the General Students' Committee (AStA). Contact details are in the AStA imprint. The AStA privacy policy applies in addition.

Data protection officer

The AStA's data protection officer is the data protection officer of the Stiftung Universität Hildesheim. You can reach him via the university's data protection page.

Legal basis

We process personal data only as far as necessary to run this service. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(e) GDPR in conjunction with § 3 NDSG and § 20(1) NHG (representing the academic interests of students). Providing your university email address is voluntary, but you cannot use the service without it.

Sign-up

When you sign up, we use your university email address to send you a personal access link via the mail server of the University of Hildesheim. We do not store your address. The link is valid without time limit and contains your email address in signed but not encrypted form, so please do not share it.

Requesting study materials

When you select modules, we create share links valid for 7 days in the student council's AcademicCloud account and send them to your university email address. Anyone who knows one of these links can access the files during that time. Your address is not stored.

Submissions (beta)

Uploaded files and the optional note are stored in the student council's AcademicCloud account and reviewed by the student council. Usable submissions are made available to other students through this service for as long as they are offered. Submissions that cannot be used are deleted after review.

We store neither your name nor your email address with submissions. Like every other request, the upload itself is recorded in the server logs (see below). Please remove personal data from the file before uploading it, including file properties such as the author name in PDF or Word files.

Recipients and hosting

Emails are sent via the mail server of the University of Hildesheim. Files are stored in the AcademicCloud, which the university provides through the GWDG (Gesellschaft für wissenschaftliche Datenverarbeitung mbH Göttingen). Our server is hosted by Hetzner Online GmbH (Gunzenhausen, Germany) in a data center in the EU. No data is transferred to countries outside the EU.

Server logs

For every request, the server logs the IP address, time, requested address, status code and your browser's user agent. Access links are masked. The logs serve secure operation and abuse prevention and are deleted automatically after 30 days. For rate limiting, the IP address is also kept in memory for up to one hour.

Usage statistics

We count how often people sign up, which modules are requested and how many files are submitted. Only monthly totals are stored, without email addresses, IP addresses or times, so no conclusions about individuals are possible.

Cookies, tracking and external content

We use no cookies and no tracking. Fonts are loaded from our own server. Your browser's storage (localStorage) only keeps your language choice.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure and restriction of processing. Please contact the AStA. You can also lodge a complaint with the data protection authority of Lower Saxony (Der Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz Niedersachsen).

Right to object

You can object at any time to the processing of your data on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21 GDPR). Please contact the AStA.
