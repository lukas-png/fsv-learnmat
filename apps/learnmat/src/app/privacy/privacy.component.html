Privacy policy

As of October 2026. The German version is legally binding.

Controller

The study material distribution is run by the Computer Science student council. The controller under the GDPR is the General Students' Committee (AStA) of the Stiftung Universität Hildesheim. Contact details are in the AStA imprint. The AStA privacy policy applies in addition.

Sign-up

When you sign up, we use your university email address to send you a personal access link via the mail server of the University of Hildesheim. We do not store your address in a database. The link contains your email address in signed but not encrypted form, so please do not share it. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the service you requested).

Requesting study materials

When you select modules, we create share links valid for 7 days in the AcademicCloud of the GWDG (Gesellschaft für wissenschaftliche Datenverarbeitung mbH Göttingen) and send them to your university email address. Your address is not stored. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Submissions (beta)

Uploaded files and the optional note are stored in the AcademicCloud and reviewed by the student council. We store neither your name nor your email address with them. Please remove personal data from the file before uploading it. Submissions that cannot be used are deleted.

Server logs

For every request, the server logs the IP address, time, requested address, status code and your browser's user agent. Access links are masked. The logs serve secure operation and abuse prevention and are deleted automatically after 30 days. For rate limiting, the IP address is also kept in memory for up to one hour. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Usage statistics

We count how often people sign up, which modules are requested and how many files are submitted. Only monthly totals are stored, without email addresses, IP addresses or times, so no conclusions about individuals are possible.

Cookies, tracking and external content

We use no cookies and no tracking. Fonts are loaded from our own server. Your browser's storage (localStorage) only keeps your language choice.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure and restriction of processing, and the right to object to processing. Please contact the AStA. You can also lodge a complaint with the data protection authority of Lower Saxony (Die Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz Niedersachsen).
